Hi! I'm Jennifer Bradley, creator of Speech Therapy Plans.

When I became an SLP, I was excited to help my students with fun, engaging therapy sessions. After a couple of years, I was stressed-out, frustrated and, honestly, felt hopeless — I couldn't find the time to plan therapy, and I was tired of spending nights and weekends away from my family searching for individualized materials.

So I created and organized therapy plans and materials for every single week of the year. Having plans that are ready to go lets me focus on why I became an SLP in the first place: engage my students, help them achieve their goals, and get them to a place where they don't need speech anymore.

I wanted other SLPs to have access to my plans and materials — and that's why Speech Therapy Plans began!
